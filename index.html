Engineering principles

Clean structure isn't an afterthought. These rules run through every codebase.

  • Consistency

    PascalCase throughout the codebase. One naming style in every script.

  • Conciseness

    Compact implementation without sacrificing structure.

  • Modularity

    Systems are isolated, reusable and intentionally structured.

  • Responsiveness

    Networking is designed around keeping latency out of the player's way.
